# How to Get Your e-RESERVE Ticket

**SIGLA 2026: A Student's Night**
November 26, 2026 · 5:00 PM – 10:00 PM · HEB Grounds

Website: **e-reserve.online**

**Who are you?** [Students](#for-students) · [Faculty, Instructors and Staff](#for-faculty-instructors-and-staff) · [Sponsors](#for-sponsors)

## Good to know

- **Cost:** Free. No payment.
- **One account:** One account gets one ticket.
- **Ticket:** You get the ticket only after the SSC approves your file(s).
- **Event:** November 26, 2026 · 5:00 PM to 10:00 PM · HEB Grounds
- **Seats:** 3,000 in total. Students 2,500 · Faculty and staff 200 · Sponsors 300.

> **Tip:** the pictures show a computer and an iPhone. Your screen may look a little different, but the buttons are the same. The **red box** shows the button to press.

---

## For Students

**What you need**

- Your school Google email (ends in @g.batstate-u.edu.ph)
- Your SR-code
- 3 PDF files, up to 5 MB each: signed waiver, COR (Certificate of Registration), school ID

Go to **e-reserve.online** and press **Get my ticket**.

## Problems and Fixes

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| SR-code not found | Check your SR-code and your college. Still not working? Message the SSC. |
| Sign-up is not open yet | Students can sign up starting on the date shown on the website. |
| No email from e-RESERVE | Check Spam. Then press **Resend the link**. |
| A file was returned | Read the reason. Fix the file. Upload it again. |
| Can't upload | Use a PDF file. Keep it under 5 MB. |
| No ticket yet | Not all your files are approved. Press **Track** to check. |
| Forgot your password | Press **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page. |
